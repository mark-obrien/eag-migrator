No database needed on either side — v2 is scraped, v3 is written through its API. The whole migration runs from this page, in order:
  1. Sign in to v2 and harvest it into staging (phases 1–2). Try Harvest with a limit of 2 first, and check what landed.
  2. Learn v3's contract. Connect to real v3 with your session cookie, then Snapshot v3 for its real ids, and one Send a record to see the payload it expects (phase 3).
  3. Write the mapping. Scaffold a draft, or open Mapping and edit it in the browser, then promote it (phase 4).
  4. Rehearse. Point at the mock, Plan, Migrate, then browse what landed and roll back. Repeat until clean (phases 3, 5).
  5. Run it. Point v3 back at production, Plan, Migrate, Verify (phase 5). Rollback is always there.
